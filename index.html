About the Event

VentureVerse 2026 is a student-focused event organised by the Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC) of JSS University, Noida. The theme of this edition is "Ideas into Action". Many students have good ideas but are not sure how to take the first step. This event is built to help with exactly that.

Over one day, you will listen to founders talk about how they started, work with other students on a real campus problem, and learn how to explain an idea clearly in a short pitch. You do not need any experience in business or startups to join.

Highlights

Learn from startup journeys: Hear real stories about starting up, including the mistakes and the lessons.
Campus Idea Sprint: Work in a team to solve a real problem on campus and present your solution.
Pitching basics: Learn how to explain your idea in a simple and confident way.
Networking: Meet speakers, mentors and students who are interested in building things.

Why Should You Attend?

  • You will understand what it takes to turn an idea into a business.
  • You will get a chance to practise teamwork and problem solving.
  • You will meet people who can guide you and may become future teammates.
  • You will learn about the activities and support the EDC offers on campus.
  • It is free for students and open to all branches and years.

FAQ

Who can attend?

Any student of JSS University. First-year students are especially welcome.

Is there any fee?

No, the event is free for students. Seats are limited, so register early.

Do I need a startup idea to take part?

No. You can join a team in the Idea Sprint and help build someone else's idea.

What should I bring?

Your student ID card, a notebook and a pen.
